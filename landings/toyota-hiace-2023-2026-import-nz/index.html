Why Kiwis choose the HiAce

NZ buyers consider the HiAce because the body style matters as much as the badge: Super GL Dark Prime II suits business use with a more finished cabin, while Grand Cabin suits passenger work with 10 seats. The scoped imports include GDH206V 2.8 diesel AWD, GDH201V 2.8 diesel RWD, TRH229W 2.7 petrol AWD and TRH224W 2.7 petrol RWD, so the right choice depends on load, seating and where the van will spend its working week. Cargo space, payload needs and condition should be matched to the exact listing, especially if you are weighing a Japan auction vehicle against NZ ex-fleet vans. If diesel dependability is your reason for looking, the auction list still needs to support the exact van's mileage, grade and notes.
