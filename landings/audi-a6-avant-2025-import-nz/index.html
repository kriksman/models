Under the skin

DTY 2.0-litre diesel turbo

The confirmed engine here is the DTY 1,968cc diesel turbo, rated at 204PS, about 201hp, and 400Nm. In a car like the A6 Avant, the torque figure matters more than the power headline because it is what gives the car that easy, low-effort surge from everyday revs. That suits the brief of a large executive wagon, especially if the car will spend time carrying adults, luggage or both.

02

7-speed S tronic

Audi's S tronic is a dual-clutch transmission, so it shifts through pre-selected gears rather than relying on the softer feel of a traditional automatic. Behind the wheel that means cleaner, quicker responses when you ask for acceleration. It is one of the reasons this diesel A6 does not feel lazy despite being tuned for torque rather than outright performance.

quattro drivetrain

quattro is a big part of why buyers look for this version instead of a front-wheel-drive wagon. The practical point is simple, it gives the car more secure traction and a more settled feel when conditions turn wet. For NZ use, that matters on long motorway trips and mixed-surface driving.
