What sets it apart03

What sets it apart

These are the parts of the A6 Avant that make it more than just a large wagon.

quattro all-wheel drive

Quattro is a core part of the A6 Avant's appeal. Audi's system varies drive between the front and rear axles according to conditions, which helps the car feel more settled when grip changes. For NZ buyers, that's useful on wet motorways and uneven rural surfaces.

7-speed S tronic transmission

Audi's S tronic is a dual-clutch gearbox rather than a conventional automatic. In plain terms, it shifts quickly and keeps the turbo engine in the right part of the rev range, so the car feels alert without needing to be worked hard.

466L luggage area

The official 466-litre boot figure matters because it tells you this is a proper wagon with useful carrying space, not just a sleek shape. The low roofline also makes loading easier than lifting gear into a taller SUV.

advanced trim

The confirmed 2025 trim name is advanced, which is the core grade to confirm when reviewing auction cars. Some listings also show extra packages, so the smart move is to confirm the exact car's lights, wheels and cabin features from the listing before bidding.

Fitted technology04

Under the skin

These are the systems worth understanding before you shortlist one.

01

ECR 2.0-litre turbo petrol

This engine is a 1,984cc inline four with turbocharging. The useful part for an owner is the 370 Nm torque figure, because that is what gives the car easy shove in normal driving rather than needing high revs all the time. In a wagon like this, that suits loaded trips and motorway work well.

02

quattro drivetrain

Quattro is Audi's all-wheel-drive system, and on this model it distributes torque between the front and rear axles according to driving conditions. What you notice from the driver's seat is cleaner traction when the surface changes and a more planted feel in poor weather.

03

7-speed S tronic

S tronic is Audi's dual-clutch transmission. It pre-selects the next ratio, which is why shifts feel quicker and more direct than a traditional torque-converter automatic. In daily use that means smoother response when you ask for acceleration, especially when joining faster traffic.

04

466L boot capacity

The official 466-litre luggage figure is a practical number worth checking because it explains why the A6 Avant works for family bags, work gear and longer trips. It gives you wagon usefulness without moving to a taller SUV body.
