What sets it apart03

What sets it apart

On a Crown, the worthwhile differences are the ones that change how easy the car is to shortlist and live with.

Two body styles under one 2022 model year

A 2022 Crown can be either the final S220 sedan or the newer S235 Crown Crossover. Getting that right early saves time and keeps your search focused on the shape and driving position you actually want.

Grade ladder with clear comfort-focused options

G, G Leather Package, G Advanced and G Advanced Leather Package give buyers several upper-trim paths before stepping into RS territory. That is useful if you want a more comfort-led Crown rather than choosing the range for its sportier badge.

Upper trims that are easy to target

The launch range gives you clear trim targets rather than one catch-all flagship. G Advanced Leather Package and RS Advanced are worth shortlisting first if you want a higher-spec car and want the search narrowed quickly.

Crossover option for easier access

The S235 Crown Crossover adds a taller body style to the Crown name. For NZ buyers, that can mean easier entry and exit, a slightly higher seating position and a different feel from the traditional sedan.

Fitted technology04

Under the skin

The 2022 Crown only makes sense once the drivetrain split is clear.

01

A25A-FXS hybrid with E-Four

This is the launch powertrain for X and the G-series Crown Crossover grades. It uses Toyota's hybrid transaxle with reduction gear rather than a conventional stepped automatic. E-Four adds an electrically driven rear axle. In daily driving that gives the car the smooth low-speed response people expect from a Toyota hybrid, with extra traction when the rear axle is called on.

02

T24A-FTS turbo hybrid with Direct Shift-6AT

RS and RS Advanced use a different idea altogether. The T24A-FTS turbo hybrid pairs a 2.4-litre turbo petrol engine with a Direct Shift-6AT, so it has a more stepped, conventional feel under load than the 2.5 hybrid setup. That is the practical difference many buyers miss when they assume every Crown hybrid drives the same.

03

E-Four versus E-Four Advanced

Toyota split the AWD systems as well as the engines. The 2.5 hybrid grades use E-Four, while the RS grades use E-Four Advanced. The key buying point is that Toyota tied each AWD system to a specific powertrain and grade family, so we confirm the exact grade and drivetrain on each car rather than relying on a broad Crown description.
